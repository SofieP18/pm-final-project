# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which Riverty business-unit case are you using?:** BU Collection: A Fair Way Forward
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** It risks losing competitiveness as other companies offer smoother digital repayment solutions, while operational costs remain high and recovery opportunities are missed.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Consumers are likely using Google, calculators, spreadsheets and their banking apps to understand their finances and find affordable repayment options, or contacting customer service because Riverty's current journey does not give them enough clarity or confidence to make a decision digitally.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Consumers struggle to find and agree on affordable repayment plans, leading to abandoned journeys, missed recovery opportunities, higher operational costs and unnecessary agent contact.
- **Value proposition, based on the initiative in your assigned Riverty case, what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** Provide consumers with a simple, transparent and personalised digital journey to set up affordable instalment plans, while helping Riverty improve debt recovery, increase self-service and reduce manual work for collection agents.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** The business risk is high enough to justify a new initiative, as poor digital instalment journeys can negatively impact recovery performance, increase operational costs and weaken Riverty's competitive position.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** This is a systemic problem rather than an edge case, as fragmented processes and unclear digital journeys create recurring challenges for both consumers and collection agents across the instalment process.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** The value proposition directly addresses the identified problem by making the instalment journey simpler, clearer and more transparent, helping consumers make informed repayment decisions without unnecessary agent support.
