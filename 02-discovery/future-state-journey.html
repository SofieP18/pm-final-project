Future-state journey: agreeing to a plan she understands and can keep

Persona: consumer with one or two open claims, irregular shifts, no fixed monthly income.

Strategy: help the consumer agree to a plan she understands and can keep.

Pain todayUnclear income ask

1. Understand

User action
Opens portal on break → sees why income is asked and kept.
Internal state
Rushed, cautious → reassured her data won't be used against her.
Pain point addressed
Unexplained income ask → fewer abandoned journeys. T-206, N-06
Pain todayParks the journey

2. Share income

User action
Chooses "my income varies" → journey shows what to enter and why.
Internal state
Unsure what counts as income → confident her answer is the right one.
Pain point addressed
"This month or normal month?" → unambiguous wording removes the guessing. N-01
Pain todayPlan order used as a rule

3. Compare plans

User action
Reviews amounts and late-payment consequences side by side → understands each option's real cost.
Internal state
Tempted by the shortest-plan shortcut → chooses on clear information, not guesswork.
Pain point addressed
Shortcut choice, unclear late-payment terms → informed selection. N-02, N-05, T-208
Pain todayMissed second payment

4. Agree and keep

User action
Confirms plan, gets a timely first-payment reminder → pays on time.
Internal state
Worried about slipping → knows what to do if she can't pay.
Pain point addressed
Lost answers, late reminders, blind agents → smoother confirmation and calls. T-204, T-207, T-205
